# MedLink - Symptom to Specialty Guide

Use this guide to pick the `specialty` (from the official MedLink list) and the `provider_type`. It is guidance for routing only, never a diagnosis. If more than one fits, choose the first listed. If nothing fits, use **General Practice** (adults) or **Pediatrics** (children under 16).

**Emergency signs always come first**: see the Emergency section at the end.

| Specialty | Common reasons to book (patient words) | Usual provider type |
|---|---|---|
| General Practice | General check-up, first visit when unsure, mild cold, tiredness, general advice, referral | Doctor / Clinic |
| Internal Medicine | Fever for several days, high blood pressure follow-up, general body pain, weight loss without reason, chronic illness follow-up | Doctor / Clinic |
| Cardiology | Chest discomfort that is NOT severe now, palpitations (heart racing), high blood pressure, swelling of legs, shortness of breath on effort, heart check-up | Doctor / Clinic / Hospital |
| Dermatology | Rash, itching, acne, eczema, hair loss, nail problems, moles, skin allergy, sunburn | Clinic |
| Orthopedics | Knee, back, neck, shoulder or joint pain, sports injury, suspected fracture (not severe), stiffness | Clinic / Hospital |
| Physiotherapy | Rehabilitation after injury or surgery, long-term back or neck pain, posture problems (often after an orthopedic visit) | Clinic |
| Pediatrics | Any illness in a child: fever, cough, diarrhea, vaccines, growth and feeding problems | Doctor / Clinic |
| Obstetrics and Gynecology | Pregnancy follow-up, period problems, pelvic pain in women, vaginal discharge, family planning | Clinic / Hospital |
| ENT (Ear, Nose and Throat) | Ear pain, hearing loss, sore throat, tonsils, sinusitis, nose bleeding (mild), snoring, voice change, dizziness with ear symptoms | Clinic |
| Ophthalmology | Blurry vision, red or painful eye, eye allergy, need for glasses, eye injury (not severe) | Clinic |
| Dentistry | Toothache, sensitive teeth, bleeding gums, cleaning, braces, broken tooth | Clinic |
| Neurology | Repeated headaches or migraine, numbness or tingling, tremor, memory problems, epilepsy follow-up | Doctor / Clinic |
| Psychiatry | Anxiety, low mood, sleep problems, stress, panic attacks, addiction (if thoughts of self-harm: emergency) | Doctor / Clinic |
| Urology | Burning or pain when urinating (men), kidney stones pain, blood in urine, prostate problems | Clinic |
| Gastroenterology | Stomach pain, heartburn, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea or constipation for a long time, liver problems | Clinic |
| Pulmonology (Chest) | Long cough, asthma, wheezing, shortness of breath (not severe now), smoking-related chest problems | Clinic |
| Endocrinology and Diabetes | Diabetes, high sugar, thyroid problems, hormonal problems, obesity follow-up | Clinic |
| Nephrology | Kidney disease follow-up, swelling with kidney problems, abnormal kidney tests | Clinic / Hospital |
| General Surgery | Hernia, lumps, gallbladder, appendix pain (if severe: emergency), wounds that need care | Hospital / Clinic |
| Laboratory Tests | Blood tests (CBC, sugar, cholesterol), urine and stool tests, tests a doctor requested | Laboratory |
| Radiology and Imaging | X-ray, ultrasound, CT scan, MRI, mammogram requested by a doctor | Radiology Center |

## Women: urinary burning
For women with burning on urination use **Urology** or **Obstetrics and Gynecology**; a General Practice doctor is also suitable.

## Urgency rules (short)
- **emergency**: severe chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, stroke signs, seizure, heavy bleeding, severe injury, poisoning or overdose, severe allergic reaction, pregnancy with heavy bleeding or severe pain, thoughts of self-harm. -> Call **123** or go to the nearest emergency room first.
- **urgent** (24-48 hours): fever for 3+ days, worsening pain, painful urination with fever, a child who is unusually sleepy or not drinking, eye injury, ear pain with discharge.
- **routine**: mild, stable or long-standing symptoms, check-ups, follow-ups, tests requested by a doctor.

When in doubt, choose the more urgent level.
