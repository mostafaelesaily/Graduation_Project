# MedLink - Frequently Asked Questions

## General
**What is MedLink?**
A web and mobile platform in Egypt to find, compare and book verified doctors, clinics, hospitals, labs and radiology centers, keep your medical history in one place, and get AI guidance to the right specialty.

**Is MedLink free for patients?**
Creating an account, searching and using the AI search are free. You pay the provider's fee (in EGP) shown on the booking, directly to the provider at the visit. There is no online payment.

**Which languages does MedLink support?**
The AI understands Egyptian street Arabic, Arabic written in English letters (Franco), standard Arabic and English. It replies in simple standard Arabic or in English. The full Arabic interface is coming soon.

**Does MedLink accept medical insurance?**
No, insurance is not supported.

**Can I do a video consultation or order medicines?**
Not yet. Telemedicine and pharmacy integration are planned enhancements.

## Account
**I did not receive the verification email.** Check spam, then use *Resend email* on the Verify Email screen, or change the address if it was typed wrong.

**I forgot my password.** Use *Forgot password* on the login screen; a reset link is sent to your email.

**Can I sign in with Google?** Yes, on both the website and the app.

## Search and AI
**How does AI search work?**
Describe what you feel in your own words. The AI suggests the suitable specialty and urgency, then shows verified providers near you, ranked by specialty match, distance, rating and price.

**Is the AI a doctor?**
No. It gives guidance only, does not diagnose and never recommends medicines. Always confirm with a doctor.

**What happens if I describe an emergency?**
The assistant tells you to call the ambulance on **123** or go to the nearest emergency room first.

**Is my data sent to the AI?**
The text you write, the earlier messages of the same chat, your area and a short summary of your own medical records (date, specialty, title, diagnosis). Your name, phone, email, national ID and exact location are never sent, and chats are not saved.

**Does the assistant remember what I said?**
Yes, within the same conversation (until 2 hours without messages). A new conversation starts fresh.

**Does the assistant read my medical history?**
Yes. When you are logged in, it reads a short summary of your own recent records (date, specialty, title, diagnosis) to suggest a better specialty. Notes and doctor names are not sent, and no other person's records are ever used.

**Does the doctor see my chat with the assistant?**
If you book right after chatting with the assistant, the doctor you booked receives a short summary of what you described (complaint, duration, symptoms and related history) so the visit is faster. Only that doctor sees it. It is not a diagnosis.

**Can I send a voice message instead of typing?**
Yes. Tap the mic in the assistant, speak for up to 3 minutes in Arabic (any dialect) or English, and tap again to send. The reply comes as text. Your recording is not saved.

**I sent too many messages and the assistant asked me to wait.**
To keep the service fast for everyone, each patient can send up to 10 messages per minute and 100 per day. Wait a moment and try again.

**What does "External" mean on a result?**
It is a real place from Google Maps that is not registered on MedLink yet, so it cannot be booked through MedLink.

**Why can't I find providers near me?**
Allow location access, or type your area (for example "Nasr City, Cairo"). MedLink is starting in a limited number of areas.

## Booking
**How do I book?** Open a provider profile, choose the service, day and free slot, confirm. See the booking workflow.

**Why is my booking "Pending"?** The provider has not accepted it yet. You will be notified when it is confirmed.

**How do I cancel or reschedule?** From *My Appointments* choose the upcoming booking and press *Cancel* or *Reschedule*.

**Will I get a reminder?** Yes, by push notification and SMS before the appointment.

**The slot I chose was taken.** Another patient booked it a moment earlier; pick one of the suggested free slots.

## Medical history
**Who can see my medical history?**
You, and only providers who have a booking with you. Every access is logged.

**Can I upload my old lab results?** Yes, from the Records / Health History screen you can upload files such as lab tests or scans.

**Who adds records after a visit?** The provider adds the visit record (title, diagnosis, notes).

## Reviews
**When can I review a provider?** Only after a completed visit with that provider.

**Why is my review not visible yet?** Reviews are checked by the admin before they appear.

## Providers
**How do I join as a provider?** Choose *Provider* on Create Account and follow the 3 steps (account, service & verification with your medical license, admin review). See provider registration.

**How long is the review?** Usually up to 48 hours; the result is sent by email.

## Support
**How do I contact MedLink?** From the Profile tab, open Help & Support.
