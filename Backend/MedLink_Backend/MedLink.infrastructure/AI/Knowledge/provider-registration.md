# MedLink - Provider Registration

Providers join MedLink in **3 steps**. Patients only see providers that passed the admin review.

## Step 1 of 3 - Create account
- On *Create Account* choose **Provider**.
- Enter: full name, **national ID (14 digits)**, email, Egyptian phone number (+20) and password, or **Continue with Google**.
- Verify the email from the link sent to the inbox.

## Step 2 of 3 - Service & verification
- **Service information**: provider type (Doctor, Clinic, Hospital, Laboratory, Radiology Center), service name, location (address / branch) and contact phone.
- **Business documents**: postal code, tax number and the **medical license** file.
  - Accepted file types: **PDF, JPG or PNG**, up to **10 MB**.
- Press **Submit for Review**. *Back* returns to the previous step.

## Step 3 of 3 - Admin review
- The screen shows **Request Under Review**.
- The admin team checks the documents, usually within **48 hours**.
- The result is sent to the provider's registered email:
  - **Accepted**: the profile goes live with a Verified badge; the provider can add services, prices, branches and time slots and start receiving bookings.
  - **Rejected**: the email includes the reason (for example an unclear license). The provider can fix the documents and apply again.

## Common questions
- *Can I receive bookings while under review?* No, the profile is hidden until it is approved.
- *How long does review take?* Up to about 48 hours.
- *My license upload fails.* Check the file is PDF, JPG or PNG and smaller than 10 MB.
- *Do I need a separate account for each branch?* No, one provider account can have several branch locations.
- *Is registration free?* MedLink offers a free plan for providers at launch.
